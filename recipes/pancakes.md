# Pancakes

Thin pancakes, great with jam, sour cream or fresh berries.

**Servings:** 4 (about 12 pancakes) · **Time:** 30 min

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 500 ml milk
- 200 g all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil or melted butter
- butter for the pan

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs, sugar and salt together in a large bowl.
2. Add about half of the milk and all of the flour, then whisk until the batter is smooth and has no lumps.
3. Whisk in the rest of the milk and the oil.
4. Let the batter rest for 10–15 minutes.
5. Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat and grease it lightly with butter.
6. Pour in a small ladle of batter and tilt the pan so it covers the bottom in a thin layer.
7. Fry for about 1 minute until the edges turn golden, then flip and fry for another 30 seconds.
8. Stack the pancakes on a plate and serve warm.
